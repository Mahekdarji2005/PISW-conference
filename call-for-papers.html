Call for Papers

The Scientific Committee invites researchers, academicians, practitioners, policymakers, industry professionals, research scholars, and postgraduate students to submit original research papers for Oral and Poster Presentations at the conference.

The conference welcomes interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary research in Social Work and Social Welfare, Sociology and Social Sciences, Humanities and Cultural Studies, Management and Human Resource Management, Education and Psychology, Gender and Women’s Studies, Health and Mental Health, Human Rights, Law and Social Justice, Community, Rural and Urban Development, Public Policy and Governance, Economics, Livelihoods and Inclusive Development, Disability and Social Inclusion, Tribal and Marginalized Communities, Environment, Climate Change and Sustainability, Corporate Social Responsibility and Social Entrepreneurship, Digital Society and Social Transformation, Peace, Conflict and Disaster Management, Development Studies, and other allied disciplines.

Submission Guidelines

Please ensure that your manuscript complies with the conference requirements before submission.

  • Submit original and unpublished research relevant to the conference theme.
  • Manuscripts should follow the conference-prescribed format and guidelines.
  • The paper should clearly present the research problem, objectives, methodology, findings, discussion, and conclusion, as applicable.
  • Both empirical and conceptual/theoretical papers are welcome.
  • Submissions must not be simultaneously under consideration by another conference, journal, or publication venue.
  • All submissions will undergo peer review as per the conference review process.
  • Authors must follow appropriate academic citation and referencing practices.

Manuscript Requirements

  • Any potential conflict of interest involving the author(s) should be disclosed in the paper prior to submission.
  • Authors must accurately present their research findings and provide an objective discussion of the significance of their results.
  • Sufficient details about the research methodology and data used in the study must be included.
  • Submitting manuscripts to multiple conference proceedings or journals simultaneously is not allowed.
  • Plagiarism, data fabrication, and image manipulation are strictly prohibited.

For complaints or concerns regarding published articles, contact us at:
conference.pisw@paruluniversity.ac.in

Research Ethics and Integrity

Authors are expected to ensure:

  • Originality and appropriate acknowledgement of all sources.
  • No plagiarism, self-plagiarism, duplicate publication, fabrication, or falsification of data.
  • Disclosure of any conflict of interest.
  • Appropriate ethical approval and informed consent, wherever required for research involving human participants or sensitive data.
  • Responsible use and disclosure of Generative AI or AI-assisted tools, in accordance with applicable conference and publisher policies.
  • All listed authors have made a substantial contribution to the research and approved the final manuscript.

Publication and Presentation

Accepted papers will be considered for Oral or Poster Presentation. Selected papers may be considered for publication in conference proceedings, edited volumes, or special issues, subject to peer review and applicable publisher/editorial requirements.

Note: Publication and indexing are subject to the policies and final decision of the respective publisher or indexing body and are not guaranteed unless officially confirmed.

Policies & Ethics

Authors & Their Responsibilities

The paper must be an original, unpublished work written in English. All authors must have made substantial contributions to the scientific work and share collective responsibility.

Peer-Review Process

All submitted papers undergo a double blind peer-review process. Papers not following the ethical policy are rejected before peer-review.

Originality & Plagiarism

Plagiarism takes many forms and is unacceptable. Manuscripts must have a plagiarism similarity index of not more than 10%, and AI-generated content is strictly prohibited.

Copyright & Accessibility

The conference proceedings offers access to the contents in the open access system on the principles of non-exclusive license Creative Commons.
