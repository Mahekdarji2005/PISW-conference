About the Conference

The conference "Reimagining Social Work: Advancing Inclusion, Equality, Education and Social Justice for a Sustainable Future" aims to provide a multidisciplinary platform for academics, researchers, social work professionals, policymakers, practitioners, students and community stakeholders to deliberate on emerging challenges and innovative approaches in social work.
