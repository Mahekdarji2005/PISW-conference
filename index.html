About the Conference

The conference "Reimagining Social Work: Advancing Inclusion, Equality, Education and Social Justice for a Sustainable Future" aims to provide a multidisciplinary platform for academics, researchers, social work professionals, policymakers, practitioners, students and community stakeholders to deliberate on emerging challenges and innovative approaches in social work.

About Faculty of Social Work

Parul University established Faculty of Social work in May 2015 with 3 courses under its ambit namely Bachelor of Social Work, Master of Social Work & Master of Social Work (Human Resource Management). The courses focuses on key importance of relevant subjects, Orientation visits, Concurrent Field work, Rural Camps, Study Tours, Field work Seminars, Communicable Skills, Viva Voce and Block Placements.

Faculty of Social Work at Parul University aims at transforming individuals by providing them a platform for gaining social work education and knowledge through interacting and closely associate with the society at large.

About Parul University

Nestled in the vibrant city of Vadodara, Gujarat, Parul University has emerged as a beacon of academic excellence and innovation. With a sprawling 250+ acre eco-friendly campus, the university is home to students from every Indian state and 75+ countries, making it one of the most culturally diverse educational destinations in the country.

Recognized for its world-class infrastructure, industry-aligned curriculum, and outstanding placement records, Parul University houses state-of-the-art research facilities, dedicated innovation and incubation centers, and a diverse range of disciplines across multiple faculties.
