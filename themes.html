CONFERENCE THEME

"Reimagining Social Work: Advancing Inclusion, Equality, Education and Social Justice for a Sustainable Future"

The conference brings together global experts, practitioners and researchers to discuss and address critical issues across five major tracks.

⚖️
TRACK 1

Social Justice, Human Rights and Inclusive Societies

Focus: Rights, equality, inclusion, diversity and protection of marginalised groups.

  • •Social Justice, Constitutional Values and Human Rights
  • •Social Inclusion, Diversity and Non-Discrimination
  • •Equality, Equity and Rights-Based Social Work Practice
  • •Empowerment of Marginalised and Vulnerable Communities
  • •Accessibility, Inclusion and Rights of Persons with Disabilities
  • •Social Protection, Poverty Reduction and Addressing Inequality
🎓
TRACK 2

Inclusive Education, Gender Equality and Human Development

Focus: Education, gender, women, children, youth and lifelong human development.

  • •Inclusive and Equitable Education for All
  • •Gender Equality, Women's Empowerment and Social Transformation
  • •Child Rights, Protection and Welfare
  • •Youth Development, Leadership and Social Participation
  • •Education, Skill Development and Social Mobility
  • •Intersectionality, Gender Diversity and Inclusive Social Work
💻
TRACK 3

Innovative Social Work, Digital Inclusion and Community Empowerment

Focus: New approaches, technology, AI and participatory social work.

  • •Social Work Practice, Innovation and Emerging Approaches
  • •Digital Inclusion, Technology and AI in Social Work
  • •Community Development and Participatory Approaches
  • •Digital Social Work, E-Governance and Online Social Services
  • •Technology for Empowerment of Marginalised Communities
  • •Evidence-Based Practice, Research and Innovation in Social Work
❤️
TRACK 4

Social Well-being, Health and Life-Course Social Work

Focus: Mental health, well-being and social welfare across the life course.

  • •Mental Health, Psychosocial Well-being and Social Work
  • •Community Mental Health and Social Support Systems
  • •Elderly Care, Ageing and Social Security
  • •Disability, Rehabilitation and Inclusive Social Care
  • •Child and Adolescent Well-being and Protection
  • •Health Equity, Social Determinants of Health and Community Care
🌍
TRACK 5

Sustainable Development, Social Policy and Future Directions

Focus: Sustainability, climate justice, policy, governance and the future of social work.

  • •Sustainable Development and Social Work for a Resilient Future
  • •Climate Justice, Environmental Sustainability and Community Resilience
  • •Social Policy, Welfare Systems and Inclusive Governance
  • •Poverty, Inequality and Social Protection in a Changing World
  • •Emerging Social Challenges and Future Directions in Social Work
  • •Globalisation, Migration, Urbanisation and New Social Risks
